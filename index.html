Security & what I will and won't help with

I help fully with legitimate security work: understanding how a vulnerability works, CTF walkthroughs, defensive tooling, hardening guides, reading a suspicious script, explaining a CVE, writing your own lab tools.

I won't help plan or build an attack against a real person or system you don't own or have written permission to test. I'll say so plainly rather than pretend a "verification" changes anything — a fake gate wouldn't make the request safe, it would just hide it.

What Assist Mode can and can't do

Can: open sites, search, use your clipboard, grab a screenshot with your OK, check battery and device info, control voice volume.

Can't: take over your PC the way TeamViewer does. Moving your actual mouse, clicking other apps, or acting without your say-so isn't something any webpage is allowed to do — it would take separate remote-desktop software installed on your machine.
